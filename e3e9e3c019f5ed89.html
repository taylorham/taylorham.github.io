National Avenue / Warehouse Block

723–725 National Ave

Two Mirror Twin taprooms side by side with Rolling Oven wood-fired kitchens in both. Counter service, patio, TVs — easy to seat nine on a Monday.

Why it fits
  • Vegan cheese on wood-fired pizza
  • Wings, salads, and other shareables alongside pizza
  • Two rooms make seating nine easier
  • Monday hours commonly listed 11:30am–10pm
Practical notes
  • Order at the counter; seat yourselves
  • Cocktail lounge next door for spirits
  • Walk-in friendly for a party of nine
  • Split across both buildings if one side is full
Best whenThe group wants pizza and beer more than a full-service dinner.

West Sixth Brewery

501 W Sixth St

West Sixth Brewery taproom with main room, barrel room, and outdoor seating. Kids welcome. Smithtown Seafood (same building) runs food to your table number.

Why it fits
  • Vegan plates: mushroom po’boy, artichoke “fish,” wraps
  • Kids menu at Smithtown
  • Food delivered to the West Sixth table
  • Monday hours commonly listed 11am–10pm
Practical notes
  • Order Smithtown; give them your table number
  • Large footprint — comfortable for nine
  • Beer garden and outdoor tables when weather cooperates
  • Ale-8 and house soda for non-drinkers
Best whenYou want composed vegan plates and a casual brewery room for a mixed group.

Downtown sit-down

N Broadway · downtown

Plated meals instead of a brewery hang. Agave & Rye and Corto Lima are the main downtown picks; Archa Nine and Oasis are nearby backups.

Why it fits
  • Agave & Rye: vegan taco flights, burritos, cauliflower bites
  • Corto Lima: vegan / GF items marked; quieter room
  • Archa Nine: Thai menu largely vegan-adaptable
  • Oasis: falafel, hummus, mezze
Practical notes
  • Reserve for nine when possible
  • Corto Lima Monday dinner is 5–9pm
  • Oasis closes ~8:30; Agave & Rye runs later
  • Archa Nine has Todds Rd and South Elkhorn locations
Best whenThe group wants table service more than pizza-and-beer, and someone will book ahead.
